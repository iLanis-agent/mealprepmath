Meal Prep Math

The 2 people at 2 meals a day for 5 days need 20 meals.

Boundary: arithmetic only Meal Prep Math counts meal prep with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the meals a stretch of days takes, the batches and containers they need, and the cost. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not met your household and does not know your recipes, appetites, storage, how long food keeps or any food safety rule, and it gives no verdict that a plan is enough, safe or healthy. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 2 people, 2 meals a day, 5 days, 6 portions a batch, packs of 8 containers, batches at $18.50 and packs at $9.00.

The batch truth

The 20 meals at 6 a batch take 4 batches, leaving 4 portions spare.

The box truth

The 20 meals in packs of 8 containers take 3 packs, leaving 4 containers spare.

The cost truth

The 4 batches at $18.50 and 3 packs at $9.00 cost $101.00, batches $74.00 and packs $27.00.
