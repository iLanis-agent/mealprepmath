Meal Prep Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts meal prep with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the meals a stretch of days takes, the batches and containers they need, and the cost. It uses no named constants. The people, the meals a day, the days, the portions a batch, the containers a pack and the prices are yours to decide and read off your own recipe and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not met your household and does not know your recipes, appetites, storage, how long food keeps or any food safety rule, and it gives no verdict that a plan is enough, safe or healthy. Every number here is your own entry.

The meals truth

Meals: the people times the meals a day times the days.

The batch truth

Batches: the meals divided by the portions a batch, rounded up, with the portions spare.

The box truth

Packs: the meals divided by the containers a pack, rounded up, with the containers spare.

The cost truth

Cost: the batches and the packs, each at their price.

Meal Prep Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
